Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

What kind of exercises are on the cards? +

Each card features a mix of exercises including stretches, bodyweight exercises, strength training, and cardio. No equipment is needed, and each exercise can be modified to your fitness level.

Can I use these cards while traveling? +

Yes! The Exercise Card Pack is compact and portable, making it the perfect fitness companion while traveling. Whether you're in a hotel room or at the park, you can easily follow along.

Are the exercises suitable for beginners? +

Absolutely! The exercises are designed for all levels. Each card includes easy-to-follow instructions, and you can adjust the intensity to your own comfort level.

How many cards are in the pack? +

The Shukuma Exercise Card Pack contains 52 cards across 4 categories: Core, Lower Body, Cardio, and Upper Body. The card number tells you how many reps to do, so every shuffle gives you a different workout.

How do I get started? +

Simply shuffle the cards, pick one, and follow the instructions. You can choose to do just one exercise or combine several cards for a longer workout session.
